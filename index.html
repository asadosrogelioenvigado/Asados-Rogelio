La Auténtica Bandeja Paisa

The Authentic Bandeja Paisa

Frijoles, arroz, carne molida, chicharrón, huevo frito, tajada de maduro, ensalada y aguacate. La esencia de la tierra paisa en un solo plato.

A legendary Colombian platter: slow-cooked red beans with pork broth, white rice, seasoned ground beef, crispy fried pork belly (chicharrón), a fried egg, sweet fried plantain, shredded cabbage salad, and fresh avocado. The definitive icon of Antioquian cuisine.

Carta de Chicharrón

Crispy Pork Belly (Chicharrón)

3 chicharrones dorados, papas a la francesa, ensalada con aguacate y patacón. La versión más generosa del clásico paisa.

Thick cuts of slow-cooked pork belly, deep-fried to a golden, crackling crunch — the pride of Antioquian cooking. Served with crispy French fries, fresh avocado salad, and a smashed fried green plantain (patacón).

🥛 Bebidas Tradicionales

🥛 Traditional Drinks

These are uniquely Colombian drinks you will not find elsewhere — part of what makes eating here a true cultural experience.

Guandolo Guandolo
A sweet, lightly fermented drink made from panela (raw cane sugar) and water, sometimes with fruit. Refreshing and uniquely Colombian.
4k
Jarra de Guandolo Guandolo Pitcher
18k
Mazamorra Mazamorra
A traditional Antioquian corn pudding-drink: slow-cooked white corn kernels in water or milk, served slightly sweet. Thick, comforting, and iconic to the region.
4k
Claro Claro
The starchy, savory-sweet liquid left from cooking the corn for mazamorra — a prized drink in Antioquia, lightly flavored and uniquely local.
4k
